# The fair dial — what the record already says about "everything at once"

Lane note, not chapter text. No issue yet.

## The proposal, as put

Three sentences, in the owner's order:

1. Entropy is usually pictured as disorder. The better picture is *all possibilities, none
   preferred* — and that, not *nothing*, is what an abyss is.

3. Our galactic entropy is 1.

## 1 · The uniform dial is the abyss, and it is already a beat

The tetrahedron chapter gives the reader one dial: a weight per line, "a setting for what each
line counts." The fairest setting is the one where every line counts the same. Nothing is
preferred, nothing is ruled out, every direction is as good as every other. That is the owner's
abyss in the book's own vocabulary, and it needs no new word: *the setting where nothing is
preferred*.

[`the-round-ripple.md`](../chapters/the-round-ripple.md) beat 4, *The obvious setting*, already
turns that dial first and reports the answer: "Set every line to the same weight and the ring comes
out **lopsided**." So the direction the owner is asking for is not a new chapter. It is a
*reading* of a beat the book already has, and the reading is stronger than the beat currently
admits.

## 2 · What the fair dial cannot do — the number the beat is missing

Rung 7.1 registered the question *can the fair dial be made round by any fair-minded adjustment?*
and got a **no**: on the rule graph, the uniform dial's cone anisotropy is exactly **3/5**, and no
positive reweighting nulls it. The ledger's own words: "entropy-flatness ≠ cone-isotropy … the
bridge survives as a dictionary, not a variational principle."

That is the sentence the owner is reaching for, computed. *Everything equally possible* does not
give you a world that behaves the same in every direction. A round world is one particular
setting, read off the geometry — a choice, which is what beat 5, *The same dial, set from the
geometry*, then makes. The book's habit of marking a choice as a choice ("That decision is not read
off the object") carries the beat.

What "special" honestly means here is **set**. The round ring is what you get when somebody sets
the dial, and the lopsided ring is what you get when nobody does. The one comparison worth making
is with *lucky*: a lucky setting would be one among many that happened to work, and this is the
one setting the shape itself reads out.

## 3 · "Static" is conservation, not stillness

The owner's second sentence is right in the toy for a reason the book already owns and can say
plainly. Once the dial is set and the world is wrapped, the one rule conserves the total exactly:
the numbers move, the sum does not. Nothing runs down, because there is nowhere for it to go and
no rule that loses any. The naive picture *time is entropy running down* was registered against
the engine's clock and refuted (UniForge `lab/0056`, cited by the napkin 0004 gate: "the arrow
must be conservative, not thermodynamic" — a pointer, not in the snapshot).

So the toy is *static* in exactly one checkable sense — a conserved sum on a world with no edge —
and it is *not* static in the sense the owner's word first suggests: the ripple moves. Any beat
must say which.

## 4 · What may not be said

This is the longer half, because the proposal's third sentence is precisely the register the last
eight proof-read rounds closed.

- **Nothing that names the world.** *Our galaxy*, *our universe*, *the abyss we came out of*,
  *why our world holds still* — every one of these is a nature subject, and round 8 of the guard
  made a nature subject veto every exemption. `our universe bubbled into existence` is a listed
  forbidden phrase. A beat about the fair dial is about *the dial*.
- **Not *entropy*.** The engine computed a spectral entropy and fenced it as "never a physical
  entropy." Bringing the word into prose invites the reader to promote a graph constant to
  thermodynamics — the exact promotion the fence exists to refuse. *The setting where nothing is
  preferred* says the same thing and cannot be misread.
- **Not *the abyss*, as a noun, in a chapter.** It is a fine word for a note. In prose it is a
  claim about what lies outside the toy, and the book's answer to "what lies outside" is a wrapped
  world with no outside.
- **Not "entropy is 1."** As arithmetic it is `0`; as a claim it names the world. Both halves fail.
- **Not a variational principle.** Rung 7.1's strong form — that flattening the spectrum *makes*
  the cone round — was falsified. The dictionary (uniform dial ↔ nothing preferred) stands; the
  mechanism (nothing-preferred *produces* round) does not. A beat that implies the second is quoting
  a refuted rung as if it passed.

## What these numbers should be spent on

**Not a new chapter, and not a change to the primer.** The book already opens on the reader
(*What you will have*) and already has the owner's abyss: *Staring into the abyss* (PR #102,
2026-09-07), a primer reflowed from the owner's own scene — the house a block from the ocean, the
void, and zero as the first mark. That primer's abyss is *Nothing — the one place a first mark
could go*. The proposal read here is a **second reading of the same scene** — the abyss as
*everything, none preferred* — and the two are different objects: a blank page, and an even
setting of a dial. The book should keep them apart. Under `EDITION_STANDARD.md` § *A primer* a
drafter reflows the owner's words and adds no experience, so if the second reading belongs in the
primer, the owner writes it there; this note only records that it is available and where the toy
already carries it.

Inserting a chapter would also shift every `## N` in `OUTLINE.md` and every appendix section, for
a beat that already has a home.

**One beat, in *Is it round?*, between beats 4 and 5 — or folded into 4.** The reader has just
turned the fair dial and seen 22.4%. The beat to add is the one she will ask for: *can't we fix that
by being fairer still?* No — and the record has the number: **3/5**, un-nullable. Then beat 5 lands
harder, because *set from the geometry* is now visibly the only route that worked, said of the
dial. The rung 7.1 sentence is at `record/PREDICTIONS.md:60` at the pinned commit, so it enters as a
`record_quote` with no record bump. A ✎ is already in the chapter; this beat sits after it.

**One sentence, in *Can a gap be emptier than empty?*** The chapter already says "empty" is a
long list of possibilities and that walls remove some. The owner's phrasing — *not nothing:
everything, none preferred* — is a tightening of beat 2, not an addition.

**Under the guard.** Every candidate sentence should be written with a toy noun as its subject
(*the dial*, *every line*, *the ring*) and run past `python3 check_edition.py` before it is
judged. The three-column self-test (about the toy / about the world / world with a toy noun parked
beside it) is the shape to draft against.

## Art — the direction, and the reference material

The thirteen untracked files under `chapters/assets/` (2026-09-05) are **reference material for
image testing, not art for the book.** The owner shot the puzzles and picked the snow-leopard
puzzle box because the AI-generated studies came back cartoonish; they show what a real object on
a real table looks like when a boundary is the subject. They are not referenced by any chapter and
must not be, as they stand:

- `snow-tiger-puzzle.jpg` and its derivatives `st1–st5.jpg` are third-party (a worldwildlife.org
  product photo). Reference only; never published. `jigsaw-boundary.svg`'s description says
  *Siberian tiger*; the animal is a snow leopard.
- `tet-oct-1..3.jpg`, `Tet-Oct-4.jpg`, `PsFractal-Puzzle-1.jpg`, `isolation-focus.jpg` are the
  owner's photographs and are the model for what replaces the studies.

**The direction to take to heart:** replacement art is *photographed or rendered real objects* —
a puzzle with its edge pieces locked first, a pyramid puzzle on a table, a jade plant in front of a
blurred room — composed under [`ART_DIRECTION.md`](../ART_DIRECTION.md)'s contract (concept, alt
text, the `Analogy — not data.` opener, a table row), with no meaning encoded in colour and the
tetrahedron drawn only by `tools/canon.py`. Two studies this note names as the first to make:

1. **The boundary study for *Where the inside shows*** — the chapter has no study drawn
   ("has not been drawn", `EDITION_STANDARD.md`). The jigsaw-edge idea is right; the photograph
   must be the owner's own puzzle, not the WWF box.
2. **The fair-dial study for *Is it round?*** — one object, lit evenly from every side, and the
   same object lit from one chosen side. Even light is the fair dial; it is not the light that
   shows the shape.

Until a replacement lands, the reference files stay untracked. They were moved out of
`chapters/assets/` to `reference/image-tests/` (2026-09-28), a folder the build does not walk and
`.gitignore` excludes, so `figures.mjs --check-rendered` never meets them by accident.
