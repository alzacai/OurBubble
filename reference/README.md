# reference/ — image-testing material, not book assets

Untracked by design (`.gitignore`). These files are what replacement art is judged against, not art
for the book: the owner's photographs of real puzzles and objects, plus third-party imagery that
must never be published. A chapter never references anything in here; a study that earns its place
goes to `chapters/assets/` with a row in `ART_DIRECTION.md`. The direction is in
`notes/the-fair-dial.md`, section "Art".
